The History

The story behind the Mid-Autumn Festival

Mooncake Festival

The Mooncake Festival, also known as the Mid-Autumn Festival, is a traditional celebration observed on the 15th day of the eighth month of the lunar calendar.

The full moon is one of the most important symbols of the festival. Its round shape represents completeness, unity, and togetherness.

One of the best-known legends connected with the festival tells the story of Chang'e, who is believed to live on the moon. The story has different versions throughout Chinese culture.

Interesting Facts

• Celebrated on the 15th day of the 8th lunar month.

• The Mandarin name is 中秋节 (Zhōngqiū Jié).

• Lanterns are traditional festival decorations.

• Mooncakes are one of the festival's best-known foods.
